# Slides and documents

The same look as the websites, for talks, PDFs and social images. Colours and fonts come from [Foundations](foundations.md); the words follow [Language](language.md).

## Slides

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920 x 1080 px.
- **Ground:** `bg` light (`#FAFBFC`) by default. Use the dark ground (`#0F1B2D`) for a whole deck in a dark room, not for single slides.
- **Fonts:** [Space Grotesk](https://floriankarsten.github.io/space-grotesk/) for all text, [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) for labels, code and the slide footer. Install both from their official pages; the files in `dist/fonts/` are for the web only.
- **One idea per slide.** A title that states it, and at most three short points.
- **Sizes:** title 64 to 80 px, text at least 32 px, labels at least 20 px.
- **Margins:** at least 96 px on every side.

### Four slide types

| Type | Content |
|---|---|
| **Title slide** | The talk's title in `ink`, a mono eyebrow with the short orange bar (event and date with day name), the speaker's name |
| **Statement slide** | One sentence, large, nothing else |
| **Point slide** | A title and up to three points, orange markers |
| **Code slide** | JetBrains Mono, at least 28 px, on `card` with a `line` border |

- **Footer** on every slide but the title slide: the speaker's name and the slide number, mono, `muted`, small.
- **Charts:** blue for the main series, orange for the one to look at. Never red. Label lines directly instead of a legend where you can.
- **Images:** pen drawings on their white `paper`. Every image in a PDF keeps an alt text.

## Documents and PDFs

- **Page:** A4, margins 25 mm.
- **Text:** Space Grotesk 11 pt, line height 1.5. Headings 600 weight, sentence case.
- **Labels and tables:** JetBrains Mono 9 pt for labels; table rules in `line`.
- **Links** in `blue`, underlined.
- **Dates** with the day name, units metric.

## Social images

- **Share card:** 1200 x 630 px, the `bg` ground, the logo or a pen drawing centred with even margins.
- **Profile picture:** the pen drawing in a circle, as on the sites.
- **No text smaller than 32 px** on an image meant for a feed.
